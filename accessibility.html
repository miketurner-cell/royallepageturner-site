Accessibility

Our commitment to an inclusive online experience

Our Commitment

Royal LePage Turner Realty is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive and welcoming online experience that allows everyone to access the information and services we offer.

Standards We Follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

What We Do

To help ensure accessibility, our website includes the following features:

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy
  • Descriptive alt text for images
  • ARIA labels for interactive elements and navigation
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and links
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and background
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and devices
  • Focus-visible indicators for keyboard navigation

Known Limitations

While we work to ensure accessibility across our site, some third-party content may not fully meet accessibility standards. We are actively working with our vendors to improve the accessibility of these components.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will work to address issues promptly.
