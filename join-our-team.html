What our technology does for you

Built here, for our agents. Everything below works today for every agent, except the one line marked "in pilot".

Leads come to you ready to call

A website or phone lead is assigned to you in Follow Up Boss with a first-call task, and the office watches response times.

Your deals in one place

The office opens your deal file from the signed agreement and files the paperwork that arrives at office@. Your deal shows in Follow Up Boss too.

Turner Agent, our agent app, is in pilot with four agents.

Your listings work online

Every listing gets its own page on our site within the hour, with photos, a spoken "Hear about this home" narration and a chat that answers buyers' questions.

Listing Text Alerts and Listing Email Alerts

Buyers who sign up hear about new listings that match their search first.

Your marketing runs itself

New-listing reels with a voiceover, the Friday open-house roundup, milestones, tips, stories and just-sold posts go out on their own, and you get a text with the link to share.

A just-sold letter, ready to print

When your deal goes firm, a print-ready just-sold letter for the neighbourhood is prepared for you.

Your sold listing updates itself

Pending and sold status flow from the deal file to the website.

Deposits and paperwork handled by the office

Deposits that reach the office are recorded in trust against your deal, and your paperwork is filed and tracked on it.
